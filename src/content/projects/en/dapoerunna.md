## What it is

Dapoer Unna is a birthday cake shop in Bekasi. The site introduces the shop,
shows its location and opening hours, and provides an ordering page.

## What's in it

- A home page with the shop profile and a call to order.
- The address on Jl. Ampera, Duren Jaya, Bekasi; open 08.00–20.00.
- Order, dashboard, and location pages.
- HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with ScrollReveal.
