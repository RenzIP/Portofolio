## What it is

DigiKampus UT is a learning management system for Universitas Terbuka.
Students use one flow to browse the course catalog, open course details, and
complete payment; lecturers and admins get their own dashboards.

## What's in it

- Separate logins for students, lecturers, and admins, with password recovery
  and OTP verification.
- Google login for lecturers.
- Student dashboard: calendar, notifications, profile, course catalog,
  checkout, and payment status.
- Built with Laravel and Blade.

The Vercel demo is no longer live. The repository is kept as an archive.
