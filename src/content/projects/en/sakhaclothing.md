## What it is

Sakha Clothing is a clothing brand that offers custom screen printing. This
site is the brand's front page: it introduces the collection, highlights
trending products, and sends visitors to the account and ordering pages.

## What's in it

- A catalog of shirts and sweaters, including custom screen-printed pieces.
- Inter-city delivery, in-store pickup, and special packaging.
- Links to login, registration, the full catalog, and the store location.
- No build step: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Bootstrap, Swiper, and
  jQuery loaded from CDNs.

## Status

The front page is still online through GitHub Pages. The
`sakhaclothing.shop` domain is no longer active, so the account and ordering
links inside it are dead.
